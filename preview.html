Vetting Notepad Safaricom Edition

Ultra-compact customer vetting notepad tailored for call-center agents and customer verification flows.

  • Material Underline Inputs: Minimalist bottom borders with floating animated labels that illuminate in Safaricom Emerald on focus.
  • Dynamic Character Guide: Displays live entered characters with remaining dashes (e.g. 4 6 _ _ _) alongside length indicators (2/5).
  • Unified Flush Alignment: Mandatory indicators and optional drag handles share a unified 18px gutter so field labels align to the exact same guide.
  • Searchable Creatable Dropdown: Fast combobox selector to switch or create vetting types (SIM Swap, PUK, Reversal) without cluttering height.
  • Floating Toast System: Smooth slide-down confirmations with auto-clear countdown and 1-click Undo.
  • Comment Suggestions: Auto-suggests and remembers previous comments, manageable in Settings.

Ctrl+K to search/change type • Enter to jump between fields • Ctrl+Enter to Copy.
