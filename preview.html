    Vetting Notepad Safaricom Edition

    Ultra-compact customer verification notepad tailored for call-center agents.

    • Positioned Right by Default: Automatically opens docked to the right edge of your browser window and continuously remembers your custom screen position and dimensions once moved.
    • 250px Compact Min-Width: Supports resizing from ultra-narrow 250px up to 700px with no horizontal overflow.
    • Segmented Masked Underline: Inputs with a defined length display subtle segmented tick-mark slots on the underline that illuminate in emerald as you type.
    • Clean Zero-Duplication: No mirrored numbers printed underneath. Only a subtle counter (7/10 • 10/10 ✓) at the top right.
    • Pass / Fail Indicators: Click ✕ or ✓ on any item to mark Fail or Pass. Appends (Passed) or (Failed) to the copied output with subtle underline tint.
    • Auto-Clear Countdown on Button: Countdown displays directly on the Clear button (Cancel (15s)). Clicking it stops the timer; typing also cancels it.
    • Creatable Select Presets: Fast keyboard-driven dropdown for switching or creating vetting types on the fly.
    • Flush Alignment & Zero Clipping: Right-side buttons are guaranteed fixed width and never clipped.

    Ctrl+K to search/change type • Enter to jump between fields • Ctrl+Enter to Copy.
